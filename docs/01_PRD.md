# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Project Name
Supply Chain Analytics

## Version
1.0

## Document Owner
Project Team

## Purpose

The purpose of this project is to build a Supply Chain Analytics platform that helps organizations monitor, analyze, and optimize supply chain operations using data analytics and machine learning techniques. The system provides demand forecasting, anomaly detection, KPI monitoring, and interactive dashboards for better business decision-making.

---

# Business Objectives

- Improve demand forecasting accuracy.
- Detect supply chain anomalies automatically.
- Reduce inventory shortages and overstock situations.
- Provide real-time business insights.
- Support data-driven decision making.

---

# Target Users

- Supply Chain Managers
- Inventory Managers
- Business Analysts
- Operations Team
- Management Team

---

# Functional Requirements

## Data Management

- Import supply chain datasets.
- Validate uploaded data.
- Clean missing and inconsistent values.
- Store processed datasets.

## Forecasting

- Generate demand forecasts.
- Compare multiple forecasting models.
- Display forecast accuracy metrics.

## Anomaly Detection

- Detect unusual sales or inventory patterns.
- Highlight abnormal demand fluctuations.
- Generate anomaly reports.

## Dashboard

- Display KPIs.
- Show sales and inventory trends.
- Display forecast charts.
- Display anomaly reports.

## Reporting

- Export reports.
- Download forecasting results.
- Generate business summaries.

---

# Non-Functional Requirements

- High Performance
- Secure Data Handling
- Easy-to-use Interface
- Modular Architecture
- Scalable Design
- Maintainable Codebase

---

# Success Criteria

- Accurate forecasting results.
- Successful anomaly detection.
- Interactive dashboards.
- Fast report generation.
- Reliable system performance.

---

# Assumptions

- Valid datasets are available.
- Users have basic analytical knowledge.
- Historical data is sufficient for forecasting.

---

# Constraints

- Forecast quality depends on data quality.
- Limited historical data may reduce prediction accuracy.
- Large datasets may increase processing time.
